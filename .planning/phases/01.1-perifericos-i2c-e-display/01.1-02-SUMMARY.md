# Phase 01.1 Plan 02: Accel-Driven Cadence Wake Summary

**MPU6050 accelerometer feeds `Cadence` a second, promote-only entry point (`onAccelWake()`) so shaking a stationary tracker flips it to MOVING in ~300ms instead of waiting ~3 GPS fixes, while GPS speed remains the sole authority for declaring "stopped".**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~25 min
- **Completed:** 2026-08-03T06:23:31-03:00
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 5 (2 created, 3 modified)

## Accomplishments
- Built `accel_sensor.h`/`.cpp`: hand-rolled raw-I2C MPU6050 driver — WHO_AM_I probe + sleep-exit write in `begin()`, a single 6-byte acceleration burst in `poll()`, and a debounced one-shot `wakeEdge()` that arms only after `DEFAULT_ACCEL_WAKE_DEBOUNCE_SAMPLES` consecutive samples deviate `>= DEFAULT_ACCEL_WAKE_THRESHOLD_G` from 1.0g; no gyro register is ever touched (D-07); all I2C failure/retry policy delegates to `i2c_bus` (D-13)
- Extended `Cadence` with `onAccelWake(uint32_t)`: a second, class-contract-documented entry point that mirrors `onGatedFix()`'s STATIONARY->MOVING bookkeeping but returns false with no effect when already MOVING — the demotion path (GPS-speed hysteresis/debounce) is completely unchanged and remains the only way to declare "stopped" (D-05)
- Wired accel polling into `main.cpp`'s superloop on `DEFAULT_ACCEL_POLL_MS`, positioned after the GPS/simTick pass; on a confirmed wake it is the sole call site of `cadence.onAccelWake()`, logs `[ACCEL] wake mag=..g streak=..` immediately before the existing `[STATE]` transition line, re-initialises the real chip (not just a flag) in `i2cRecoverTick()`'s ACCEL case, and extends `[HEALTH]` with `accel_g=`/`accel_wakes=` while preserving every field from plan 01
